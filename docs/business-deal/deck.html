CARDS · MONEY · WILD · RENT

Money, wild & rent cards

Banknote style with guilloche border and Ashoka-chakra watermark. Wilds use a rangoli ring over a split-colour face. Rent cards carry a lotus mandala.

Money · 20 cards ₹57L total – ₹1L×6, ₹2L×5, ₹3L×3, ₹4L×3, ₹5L×2, ₹10L×1

Wild properties · 11 – Dark Blue/Green, Green/Railways, Utilities/Railways, Light Blue/Railways, Light Blue/Brown, Pink/Orange ×2, Red/Yellow ×2, Multicolour ×2 (₹0, cannot pay)

Rent · 13 – Two-colour ×10 (charge all players) and Wild ×3 (charge one player)

CARDS · 34 ACTIONS

Action cards

Bold gradient faces, large emoji-style icon, one-line effect. Any action can be banked as money instead.

Sizes in-app: hand cards 132×190 dp, opponent mini cards 62×88 dp. Long-press any card to enlarge (same as the QR enlarge pattern).

Turn rules
① Draw 2 cards (5 if hand was empty)
② Play up to 3 cards: bank, property, or action
③ End turn; discard down to 7
④ Payments: only table cards, no change, can't pay with hand cards
⑤ Complete sets are protected from Sly/Force Deal; only Deal Breaker takes them
⑥ Just Say No can be countered by another Just Say No, endlessly
⑦ First to 3 complete sets wins · empty draw pile reshuffles discards
⑧ Turn timer expiry auto-ends the turn (hand limit enforced)
