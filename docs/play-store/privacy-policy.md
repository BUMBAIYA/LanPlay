# LanPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

LanPlay is a local multiplayer card game app by BUMBAIYA. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## Summary
LanPlay does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## What the app stores on your device
- The display name you type in Settings, Host or Join. It stays on your phone.
- Your preferences: sound, vibration and screen orientation.

## What is shared with other players
When you host or join a room, your display name and your game moves are sent over your local Wi-Fi network or hotspot to the host and the other players in that same room, so the game can work. This data goes directly between the phones. It is not sent to the developer or to any server on the internet.

## What the app does not do
- It does not use an account, login or sign-up.
- It does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or advertising ID.
- It does not include advertising or third-party analytics or tracking.
- It does not send any information to the developer.

## Permissions
The app asks for network permissions to run a game on your local network, a foreground service and notification permission to keep a hosted room running, and vibration for turn alerts. Scanning a QR code to join a room uses Google's code scanner from Google Play services. See the Google Play services privacy policy for how that component works.

## Children
LanPlay is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Deleting your data
Nothing is stored on any server. Uninstalling the app removes your saved name and preferences from your device.
